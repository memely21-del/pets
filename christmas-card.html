How to share or print it

Share it: on your private page, tap “Share the card” to send it by text, WhatsApp or social media, or tap “Download the card” to save it.

Print it: the card is sized for a 4x6 in print. Choose a 4x6 print on your home printer (photo paper looks best), or upload the downloaded file to the photo service of a store near you and pick 4x6. Check the store's pickup time before you order.

Plan ahead: portraits are usually ready within 48 hours, so send your photo a few days before you need the card.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The portrait and the Christmas card are free. We paint up to 50 each week; when those spots are taken, the form closes for a few days.

Can I print the card?

Yes. The card is sized for a 4x6 in print. You can print it at home or send it to a photo store.

Which name goes on the card?

The name you type for your pet. Use English letters: letters or symbols our font doesn't have (like some accents or emoji) are left out.

Is it made with AI?

Yes. Portraits are made with AI tools from your photo, and our system checks the likeness before they are delivered. If it doesn't look like your pet, you can try again with another photo.

What happens with my photo?

We only use it to paint your portrait and delete it about 30 days after your order. See our privacy notice.
