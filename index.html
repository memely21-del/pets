Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The phone-size portrait is free. The print-ready version is optional.

How long does it take?

Within 48 hours, on the private link you get when you send your photo. When the 50 spots are taken, the form closes until next week.

Is this made with AI?

Yes. Portraits are created with AI tools from your photo and checked for likeness before they are delivered.

What if it doesn't look like my pet?

Write to retratos@emelytugc.com with your private link and we will redo it.

Which photos work best?

One pet per photo, face clearly visible, good light, no people in the picture.

What do you do with my photo?

We only use it to paint your portrait. Our AI provider (OpenAI) processes it for that, and we remove its hidden location data first. We delete it 30 days after delivery and we never sell it. See our privacy notice.
