Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The phone-size portrait is free.

How long does it take?

Usually within 48 hours, and often much sooner, on the private link you get when you send your photo. When the free spots are taken, the form closes for a few days until spots free up again.

What is the free Christmas card?

Your pet's watercolor portrait in a holly frame with “Merry Christmas from” and your pet's name. Download it or share it from your private page. No shipping, so it's never late.

Is this made with AI?

Yes. Portraits are created with AI tools from your photo and checked for likeness before they are delivered.

What if it doesn't look like my pet?

Open your private link and tap “Not my pet? Try again”. Then you can send another photo.

Which photos work best?

One pet per photo, face clearly visible, good light, no people in the picture.

What do you do with my photo?

We only use it to paint your portrait. Our AI provider (OpenAI) processes it for that, and we remove its hidden location data first. We delete it about 30 days after your order (fully erased from our cloud trash within 30 more days) and we never sell it. See our privacy notice.
