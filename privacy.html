Privacy notice

Last updated: September 26, 2026

What we collect

  • The photo of your pet you upload.
  • Your pet's name, your email address and, if you choose, your Instagram username.
  • Your choices: whether you want to receive offers and whether we may share your portrait.

What we use it for

  • To create your watercolor portrait and show it to you on your private link.
  • If you checked the box, to send you occasional offers by email. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • If you checked the box, to show your pet's portrait on our social media. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

How long we keep it

Your pet's photo and portrait files are removed 30 days after delivery and permanently erased within 30 more days. Your email stays on our list only if you agreed to receive offers, until you unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We only share it with the services that run this: Google (where your order and files are stored privately) and OpenAI (the AI that paints your portrait from your photo). Your browser removes the photo's hidden location data before it is sent.
