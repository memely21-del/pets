Privacy notice

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What we collect

  • The photo of your pet you upload.
  • Your pet's name, your email address and, if you choose, your Instagram username.
  • Your choices: whether you want to receive offers and whether we may share your portrait.

What we use it for

  • To create your watercolor portrait and show it to you on your private link.
  • If you checked the box, to send you occasional offers by email in the future. Every offer email will include an unsubscribe link.
  • If you checked the box, to show your pet's portrait on our social media. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

How long we keep it

We delete your pet's photo and portrait, and every copy we made to paint it, about 30 days after your order. Files in our cloud trash are erased automatically within 30 more days. If you didn't agree to receive offers, 30 days after your order we replace your email with an irreversible code (so each person gets one free portrait) and delete your Instagram username. After that we keep only that code and basic order details with no personal data: your pet's name, dates, the order status and anonymous usage counts (such as whether the portrait was viewed or downloaded). If you agreed to receive offers, we keep your email until you unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We only share it with the services that run this: Google (where your order and files are stored privately) and OpenAI (the AI that paints your portrait from your photo), which handles it under its own privacy policy. Your browser removes the photo's hidden location data before it is sent.
