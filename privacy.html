Privacy notice

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What we collect

  • The photo of your pet you upload.
  • Your pet's name, your email address and, if you choose, your Instagram username.
  • Your choices: whether you want to receive offers and whether we may share your portrait.
  • If you arrived through one of our links that carries a short campaign code (for example ?g=g2clas), that code, so we know which of our posts people find useful. It is kept with the record of your request for as long as that record exists, and on its own it does not identify you.

We count anonymous page visits (no cookies, no personal data) to know which links people use.

What we use it for

  • To create your watercolor portrait and show it to you on your private link.
  • If you checked the box, to send you occasional offers by email in the future. Every offer email will include an unsubscribe link.
  • If you checked the box, to show your pet's portrait on our social media. You can ask us to remove it at any time.
  • If you buy the print-ready version, Stripe processes your payment. We never see your card number; Stripe shares with us the payment confirmation and the name and email you enter at checkout, which we use only for your order and for support. Stripe may collect device and connection details to prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We delete your pet's photo and portrait, and every copy we made to paint it, about 30 days after your order (if you buy the print-ready version, your portrait and files stay 60 days: see below). Files in our cloud trash are erased automatically within 30 more days. If you didn't agree to receive offers, 30 days after your order we replace your email with an irreversible code (so each person gets one free portrait) and delete your Instagram username. After that we keep only that code and basic order details with no personal data: your pet's name, dates, the order status and anonymous usage counts (such as whether the portrait was viewed or downloaded). If you agreed to receive offers, we keep your email until you unsubscribe. If you buy the print-ready version, we keep your portrait, card and print files for 60 days after your order (at least 30 days after your purchase) so you can download them again, and we keep the payment record (order number, amount and date) as long as needed for accounting and payment disputes.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We only share it with the services that run this: Google (where your order and files are stored privately), OpenAI (the AI that paints your portrait from your photo) and, if you buy, Stripe (payments), each under its own privacy policy. Your browser removes the photo's hidden location data before it is sent.

Terms of sale

The print-ready version costs $9 (USD) and is a digital download: two JPG files of your pet's portrait (one with your pet's name on a paper border, one in a Christmas frame) for your personal use. Nothing is shipped. After payment your files appear on your private page, usually within an hour. If they are not there within 48 hours, write to us and we will refund you. They stay there for at least 30 days after your purchase, so please download them. Payments are processed by Stripe. Seller: Emely Toledo, Florida, USA.

Refunds

If something is wrong with your print-ready files, write to us within 14 days of your purchase with your private link and we will fix it or refund you.
